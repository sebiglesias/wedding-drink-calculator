Frequently Asked Questions

How many glasses of wine are in a 750ml bottle?

A standard 750ml wine bottle yields exactly 5 glasses of wine at the standard 5-ounce restaurant pour.

How much ice do I need for a party?

Plan for 1.5 lbs of ice per guest for warm weather or outdoor events (covers both chilling beer/wine in tubs and drink ice). For indoor winter dinner parties, 1 lb per guest is sufficient.
